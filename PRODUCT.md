# AryaTeja.com

## Register

brand

## Users

Arya's warm US connections, founders, operators, and small teams exploring practical AI agent systems. They arrive with limited time and a trust question: can Arya understand a real business problem, work remotely from India, and ship a secure, useful system?

Secondary users are collaborators, open-source builders, recruiters, search engines, and software agents trying to understand Arya's identity, current work, writing, and availability without relying on stale profiles.

## Product Purpose

AryaTeja.com is Arya Teja Rudraraju's primary trust and conversion surface. It should quickly explain what he is building, show verifiable evidence from real work and real communities, publish useful writing, and help a qualified visitor share a problem or schedule a conversation.

The site is the source of truth for Arya's current public profile. CloudAGI is a related consulting domain being repositioned, not the primary identity or conversion path. The current focus is direct consulting through AryaTeja.com, especially secure agent setup, local-first systems, remote access, workflow audits, and business-first AI implementation.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, capable, generous.

The voice is business-first and technically credible. It shows work, explains tradeoffs, and teaches without pretending experiments are finished products. It should feel human and current, never staged or inflated.

## Anti-references

- Do not call the site a public archive.
- Do not present Arya as an AI Product Manager, aspiring candidate, Dallas-based operator, or job seeker.
- Do not reuse CloudAGI's abandoned blockchain or crypto positioning.
- Do not lead with a giant tech stack, neon terminal effects, generic robot imagery, or an AI-generated developer-portfolio aesthetic.
- Do not publish unverified metrics, testimonials, project status, or security claims.
- Do not make visitors navigate several brands before they understand how Arya can help.

## Design Principles

1. Show proof before claims. Use real photos, working demos, repositories, screenshots, commands, and clearly sourced writing.
2. Make the next step obvious. A qualified visitor should be able to share a problem, inspect relevant work, or schedule a call without hunting.
3. Write for people and agents. Keep content semantic, stable, accessible, and backed by truthful structured metadata.
4. Keep business value ahead of technical novelty. Explain the problem, constraints, and outcome before the stack.
5. Treat trust as the product. Mark experiments honestly, keep stale facts out of public metadata, and make security boundaries visible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic landmarks, descriptive alternative text, sufficient contrast, responsive text, large mobile targets, and a reduced-motion experience. Do not rely on color, animation, or hover alone to convey meaning.
