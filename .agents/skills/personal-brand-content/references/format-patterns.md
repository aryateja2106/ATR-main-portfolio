# Format Patterns

## LinkedIn

Best for narrative plus credibility.

Structure:

1. Hook from a real build or source.
2. Short context.
3. What changed or what you learned.
4. 3 to 5 practical takeaways.
5. Closing question or invitation.

Keep it grounded. One screenshot, carousel, or diagram should carry the visual.

## X

Best for a sharp point or compact thread.

Standalone:

- First sentence should contain the claim.
- Second sentence explains why it matters.
- Third sentence gives the practical move.

Thread:

- 1: hook.
- 2 to 4: evidence or steps.
- 5: takeaway.
- 6: source link or build log.

## YouTube Short

Best for one visible idea.

Structure:

- Hook: 0 to 3 seconds.
- Context: 3 to 10 seconds.
- Demo or visual: 10 to 40 seconds.
- Takeaway: 40 to 55 seconds.
- Close: 55 to 60 seconds.

Avoid multi-topic scripts. Show one screen, one diagram, or one before/after.

## Carousel

Best for LinkedIn and X reuse.

Use 3 to 7 slides:

1. Claim.
2. Context.
3. Mechanism.
4. Example.
5. Risk or mistake.
6. Takeaway.
7. Follow or source.

Use one claim per slide. Prefer diagrams, terminal snippets, repo screenshots, and annotated workflows.

## Blog

Best for durable site content and AEO.

Structure:

1. What I tried.
2. Why it matters.
3. The system.
4. What broke.
5. What I would do again.
6. Sources and next steps.
